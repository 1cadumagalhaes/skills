extra · canvas texture

A Rubik's cube as living ASCII

The cube is drawn once in isometric projection, turned into a height field by blurring it three times and multiplying back by its own alpha, lit with a fake normal, and re-drawn as glyphs from a pre-baked atlas. Each sticker becomes a small dome; the three faces carry a different alpha, which is what separates them in the light. Move the pointer across it: the light follows.

8 × 11 px cell · 24 fps · 10 glyphs × 5 tones · campo de altura de 300 × 150 · a forma vem de data-forma no canvas (cubo ou infinito)
